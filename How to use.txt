VIDEO DOWNLOADER - HOW TO USE
=============================

FIRST TIME
1. Right-click the zip file > "Extract All". (Do not run it from inside the zip.)
2. Put the "Video Downloader" folder somewhere easy, like Documents or Desktop.
3. Double-click "Start Video Downloader.bat".
   If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
   click "More info" > "Run anyway". This happens only the first time.
4. The app asks to download its tools (about 150 MB, one time only).
   Click "Yes" and wait until it says "Setup finished".

CHECK THE VERSION
- The window title shows the version, for example "Video Downloader (version 9)".
- Opening a new version once also fixes your Desktop shortcut to point to it.

DESKTOP SHORTCUT (optional)
- Double-click "Make Desktop Shortcut.bat" once.
- A "Video Downloader" icon appears on your Desktop. Use it from now on.
- Keep the "Video Downloader" folder where it is, or the shortcut stops working.

EVERY TIME
1. Open the video in your browser (it must be approved on NetFree).
2. Copy the link from the address bar.
3. In the app, click "Paste".
4. Choose "Video (MP4)", "Sound only (MP3)" or "Sound - best quality".
   Best quality keeps YouTube's own sound file (.opus, or .m4a), with no loss.
5. Click "Download".
6. When it says Done, click "Open folder".

Files save to:  Downloads\Video Downloads   (you can change this in the app)

DOWNLOAD BUTTON IN CHROME (the main way)
One time:
1. Double-click "Add Download Button to Chrome.bat".
2. On Chrome's Extensions page: turn ON "Developer mode", click
   "Load unpacked", choose the "extension" folder.
Every time:
1. Open the YouTube video in Chrome (only works on videos NetFree opened).
2. Click Video, MP3 or Best sound in the Download box at the bottom right.
3. The app opens by itself and downloads. No pictures anywhere.

IF A DOWNLOAD FAILS
- Video not approved on NetFree yet: open it in the browser and click
  "send the video for review". Try again after it is approved.
- If many videos stop working: click "Update downloader" in the app.

WHAT SETUP DOWNLOADS (into app\tools)
- yt-dlp.exe   - the downloader
- deno.exe     - needed by yt-dlp to read YouTube pages
- ffmpeg.exe, ffprobe.exe + .dll files - join video and sound, make MP3s
All free, open-source programs.

Downloads run on your own internet connection, so NetFree still filters
everything. Only download videos you are allowed to (for example, where
the owner permits it).
